## Documentation Approach

My approach combines **technical writing principles with software development practices**.

```text
Plan
  ↓
Author
  ↓
Version Control
  ↓
Review
  ↓
Automate
  ↓
Build
  ↓
Publish
  ↓
Improve
```

The goal is to treat documentation as a maintained engineering asset rather than a static deliverable.
